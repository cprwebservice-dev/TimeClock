V6.15.29 FIX15N — Borrow Public Holiday Visibility

Root cause
- Monthly Personal used canonical employee schedule rows as the only holiday source.
- Borrow destination schedule access is intentionally effective-date scoped.
- Outside the Borrow window there is no employee schedule row, so Public Holiday
  metadata disappeared together with the schedule row.

Fix
- Add read-only company holiday calendar RPC: ta_get_calendar_holidays_v61529f15n.
- Monthly Personal loads company holidays independently from employee Schedule Scope.
- Public Holidays remain visible/listed/filterable on locked out-of-Borrow dates.
- Locked dates still do NOT expose source Manager schedule/shift data and remain non-editable.
- Holiday KPI/list now use the company calendar even when every date is outside Borrow Scope.

Security
- No employee/team/schedule data is exposed by the holiday RPC.
- authenticated only; anon remains blocked.
