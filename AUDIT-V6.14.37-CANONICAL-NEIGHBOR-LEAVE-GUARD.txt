AUDIT — TimeAttendance V6.14.37
Canonical Neighbor State + Full-Day Leave Aware Night Guard

ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED
- Monthly Personal/Attendance can display a FULL-DAY LEAVE from Attendance/Leave overlay.
- Structural Schedule may still carry its underlying default shift (e.g. S043/STD) for that same date.
- V6.14.36 Night Sequence local/backend checks classified only the structural shift code.
- Result: calendar showed "ลา" but guard could report "วันก่อนหน้าเป็น S043" and falsely block Night.
